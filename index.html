Mapping Energy Futures

What do people in Kazakhstan’s coal and oil towns picture for 2050, and should AI be used to find out?

Verdict in one line

Only under conditions.
  • A local, open-weight model.
  • No raw audio or identifiable images leave Kazakhstan.
  • No automated emotion inference.
  • Humans write the codebook.
  • Run a pilot first.
  • Publish aggregate results only.
  • Ethics-approved consent that names AI processing.

Rejected, and why: a data-silence grid (its purpose was unclear); the household-income and “who can’t afford energy” rows (no direct measure of energy poverty, and the income unit was confusing); a three-panel wage figure (too complicated for a reader); the first palette (too dark and too green); titles that claimed more than the data show.
